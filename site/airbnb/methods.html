How the earlier assignments moved from questions to recommendations

Reviewed: 5 November 2026. Purpose: reuse the research method for Project 8, rather than invent another generic roadmap. This is a review of local artifacts, not a claim that every planned activity was executed or every recommendation was validated.

The reusable sequence

Understand product and business → understand market and alternatives → define a research question → gather suitable evidence → reconcile hypotheses → frame and prioritise the problem → compare responses → define tests and measures → communicate the recommendation

The order is iterative. An initial question guides desk research; stronger evidence can change the segment, problem and proposed response. Understanding a product is different from endorsing its claims, and understanding a market does not establish willingness to pay.

What we merge into Project 8

  1. Start with Airbnb's product, participants, business model and existing India presence.
  2. Study Indian travel demand, alternatives, host/provider supply and operating readiness.
  3. Compare what Airbnb can enable with what a specific travel situation needs. Produce candidate opportunities, not a final feature commitment.
  4. Choose a working segment/job and research it with methods matched to the uncertainty.
  5. Reconcile desk research, observations and firsthand input; retain contradictions and sample limits.
  6. Explain the selected problem before designing cross-functional actions, product loops, MVPs and monetization.

The merged research roadmap and dashboard implement this sequence. No earlier project's conclusions, participant records or metrics are copied into Airbnb findings.
