Updated: 5 November 2026. The requester approved the 14-stage structure after reviewing the approach. The assignment requirements are unchanged; these stages organise the work, not add grading requirements.

Submission deadline: 7 November 2026, 12:00 p.m. IST, subsequently confirmed by the requester. The original deadline was not supplied; the remaining portal/rubric details are still open.

Start with the convergence

What Airbnb enables × what Indian travel situations need × what hosts/providers can reliably deliver → candidate opportunity → research → problem choice → growth strategy → MVP learning → monetization

The previous roadmap selected a traveller before establishing enough product and market context. This revision adds the missing discovery work while retaining the existing roadmap's research, design and submission stages. The method is grounded in the review of Projects 1–7.

Airbnb already operates in India: its September 2025 India report announcement describes guest and host activity in the country. Frame the research around adoption, repeat value, relevant expansion or underserved opportunities. Do not presume that the company is entering India for the first time.

Research methods: reuse what worked

UncertaintyMethod and questionsInterpretation limit
What is the product and current India baseline?Official product/help material and accessible India journey checks; record date, device/account state and destination.A published capability or one observed listing does not prove broad availability or effective use.
How does the market work and what are the alternatives?Government/public studies and a same-lens comparison of relevant platforms and offline options.Category totals and vendor studies are context, not our target market or validated user demand.
Why do travellers choose, avoid or abandon an option?Neutral conversations about a recent concrete trip: trigger, research, alternatives, booking decision, costs, friction and workaround.Small/convenience samples reveal patterns to investigate; they do not establish market prevalence.
Can hosts/providers deliver the proposed value?Targeted supply-side conversations or documented operating evidence.Traveller enthusiasm does not establish supply feasibility. Missing host access stays explicit.
How common is an emerging pattern?A focused survey after initial discovery, or actual analytics if available. Keep Airbnb users, non-users and travel pools distinguishable.No universal sample target. Denominators, recruitment, eligibility and scope must be visible.
Where does the product lose users?Define discovery, consideration, confirmed booking, completed stay and return; request relevant event data if available.A proposed event plan is not measured behaviour. A funnel cannot establish the cause of loss alone.
Would a response help?Use a concept/prototype for comprehension and usability; a suitable live experiment for behavioural or commercial impact.Prototype feedback is not conversion uplift; a scenario is not realised revenue.

Research materials must anonymise participants and preserve only what is necessary for reasoning. This roadmap does not authorise contacting anyone, collecting sensitive data or purchasing access.

A defensible recommendation states: the chosen traveller/job; the supporting evidence and its limits; why this problem takes priority; alternatives considered; how the mechanism creates user value and growth; what must be tested; the cost/operating burden; and what would make us stop or change direction. Include the option to improve an existing workflow or defer a new feature.
