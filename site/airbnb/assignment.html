Product-Led Growth Strategy for Airbnb in India

Project 8. Assignment received: 2 November 2026. The requirements are preserved from the supplied assignment. The current roadmap supports research-led work; no segment or solution has been selected.

Context

Airbnb aims to support an end-to-end travel journey across accommodations, local experiences and trip logistics. Personal connections and community between hosts and guests are central to the positioning in the assignment.

Required work

  1. Propose PLG initiatives for Airbnb growth in India covering both cross-functional enablement (Sales, Marketing, Support, Operations and others) and product-embedded growth loops / flywheels for new and existing users.
  2. Design an MVP for every substantial feature in the product-growth section, including explicit hypotheses / assumptions, inexpensive prototypes or solutions, and success measures.
  3. Propose a new revenue model or improvements to the current model for India.
  4. Support recommendations with research, assumptions, frameworks and references. The submission must reflect the learner's reasoning, structure and originality; AI can assist brainstorming and organisation.

Step 1 — Build PLG-led initiatives

Focus on both:

  • Helping cross-functions such as Sales, Marketing, Support and Operations succeed in driving growth.
  • Building product-embedded growth loops and flywheels that support new and existing user retention.

Step 2 — Design MVPs for substantial features

For any substantial features proposed in the product-growth section, include:

  • The hypotheses and assumptions being tested.
  • The inexpensive prototypes or solutions that will be built.
  • Measures of success.

The supplied wording refers to features in “Section 2.” The working interpretation is the product-embedded initiatives from Step 1's second focus area. The brief does not prescribe a feature count or define “substantial.” Keep the MVP coverage explicit when features are eventually chosen.

Step 3 — Design or update the revenue model

Design a new revenue model, or improve the existing model, to help Airbnb better monetize users in India. The assignment does not mandate a subscription, a fee increase or any particular pricing mechanism.

Step 4 — Package the final deliverables

The single detailed PDF must contain:

  • Cross-functional PLG initiatives.
  • Product-embedded growth loops and flywheels.
  • MVP designs for substantial features, including hypotheses, assumptions, prototype approach and success metrics.
  • The proposed revenue model or improvements for India.
  • Supporting research, assumptions, frameworks and references.

Also provide the five-slide presentation converted to PDF, the presenter-visible MP4 and its Loom or Google Drive link, as specified below.
