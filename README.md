# Airtribe AI-First Product Management Portfolio

This public repository brings together coursework, breakout-room activities, learning notes, and supporting documentation from an AI-first product-management program.

The work is shared for peer learning. Each project keeps its evidence limits visible: observations, sourced facts, hypotheses, recommendations, and unknowns are not treated as the same thing.

## Repository map

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`projects/`](projects/) | Assignment workspaces, research, drafts, and submission artifacts |
| [`breakout-room/`](breakout-room/) | Time-boxed cohort exercises and decision packs |

Course-wide learning and repository guidance remain available as root documents: [`LEARNING.md`](LEARNING.md), [`DOCUMENTATION.md`](DOCUMENTATION.md), and [`PUBLIC_SHARING_CHECKLIST.md`](PUBLIC_SHARING_CHECKLIST.md).

## How to read the work

1. Open the relevant project README.
2. Read the assignment or case brief before the recommendation.
3. Check the stated research cut-off and evidence boundary.
4. Treat proposed features, pilots, and strategies as recommendations—not validated market truth.

## Public-use boundary

- This is coursework and learning material, not production or employer documentation.
- Interview material is anonymized and intentionally excludes participant and employer names.
- Product names and screenshots belong to their respective owners and are used for analysis and education.
- External facts can become outdated; follow the cited source and date before reusing a claim.
- No licence for reuse or redistribution is granted by the absence of a licence file.

See [`PUBLIC_SHARING_CHECKLIST.md`](PUBLIC_SHARING_CHECKLIST.md) before adding new public material.
