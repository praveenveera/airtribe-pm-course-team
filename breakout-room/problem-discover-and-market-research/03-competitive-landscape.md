# Competitive Landscape — India

**Case baseline:** Uber Go is the hypothetical existing two-wheeler rider service. Real-world Uber Bike/Moto information is not treated as evidence about the case service.

| Service | Features & capabilities | Price | Reviews / adoption |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Uber Go — case baseline** | Existing two-wheeler rider service; coverage, performance, safety features and EV support are not provided | Current fare and petrol-versus-EV economics are not provided | Internal rides, ratings, active users and retention are not provided |
| **Rapido Bike** | Bike-first network; trip sharing, number masking, SOS, captain details, ratings and extra helmet; EV support is not publicly established | Route/time-dependent app price; no current standard national fare | Company claims 5m+ daily rides across all categories and 70% bike-taxi share in April 2025; no comparable bike-only review score [S11][S12] |
| **Ola e-Bike** | EV bike service; 2024 launch claimed three cities, 200 Bengaluru chargers and 10,000 planned vehicles; current delivery is unverified | Historical launch offer: ₹25/5 km, ₹50/10 km and ₹75/15 km—not a current quote | Ola claimed 1.75m pilot rides; current usage and bike-only reviews are not disclosed [S17] |
| **Bharat Taxi Bike** | Driver-owned cooperative with Bike Didi; EV support is not disclosed | Reported no in-app platform, convenience or commission fee in June 2026; passenger fare is unknown | Official snapshot: 990,082 registered customers, 300,000+ registered drivers and 291,665 rides across all modes; registered does not mean active [S18][S19] |
| **Yulu — adjacent** | Self-ride EV, keyless access, battery subscription and swapping; not a chauffeured bike taxi | Subscription/self-ride pricing is not directly comparable | EV-operations benchmark; no comparable rider-service adoption or review measure [S27] |

## Result

- **Rapido:** strongest adoption signal.
- **Ola e-Bike:** clearest direct EV competitor, but the public evidence is old.
- **Bharat Taxi:** strongest driver-cost proposition at the reported snapshot.
- **Yulu:** infrastructure benchmark, not a direct competitor.
- **Uber Go:** insufficient internal evidence for a full EV conversion decision.
