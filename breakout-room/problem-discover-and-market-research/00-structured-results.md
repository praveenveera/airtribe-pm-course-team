# Uber Go EV Decision — Structured Results

**India | Research cut-off: 13 September 2026**

## 1. TAM, SAM, SOM — rides in India

| Market level | Result | Meaning | Confidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **TAM** | **280m–782m bike-taxi rides/year** | India-wide demand. The lower number is a 2022 KPMG estimate; the upper number is a claim-based planning ceiling, not a forecast. [S05][S11][S12] | Medium–Low |
| **SAM** | **10m EV bike-taxi rides/year** | **Planning assumption:** about 27,400 rides/day across selected legal, EV-ready metro catchments. This is 1.3%–3.6% of the TAM range. | Low — assumed |
| **SOM** | **1m Uber Go EV rides/year** | **Planning assumption:** 10% of SAM, or about 2,740 rides/day across the targeted metros. | Low — assumed |

### Assumptions and limitations

- All values use **rides per year**; global ride-hailing or all-two-wheeler volumes are context, not the India TAM.
- The 10m SAM is a top-down assumption. It is not supported by current city-level EV ride data.
- The 1m SOM assumes Uber Go captures 10% of SAM. It is not supported by Uber Go's current market share or supply capacity.
- State legality, charging coverage, driver adoption, seasonality and competitor reactions could materially reduce both numbers.
- Before an investment decision, replace the assumptions with city-level rides, eligible service zones, active EV drivers and expected rides per driver per day.

## 2. Porter's Five Forces

| Force | Strength | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Rivalry** | Very high | Rapido leads bike-category awareness; Ola competes directly with e-Bikes; price and availability are key battlegrounds. |
| **Supplier power** | High | Drivers can use multiple apps. EV supply also depends on vehicle, finance, charging and maintenance partners. |
| **Buyer power** | High | Riders can switch apps easily and usually compare fare and pickup time. |
| **Threat of substitutes** | High | Auto-rickshaws, metro, buses, walking, personal two-wheelers and self-ride EVs solve the same short-trip job. |
| **Threat of new entrants** | Medium | Building an app is possible; building legal access, driver density, safety operations and reliable EV support is harder. |

## 3. Competitive Landscape

| Service | Features & capabilities | Price | Reviews / adoption |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Uber Go — case baseline** | Existing two-wheeler rider service. Coverage, service performance, safety features and EV support are not provided in the case. | Current fare and petrol-versus-EV economics are not provided. | Internal rides, ratings, active users and retention are not provided. |
| **Rapido Bike** | Bike-first network; trip sharing, number masking, SOS, captain details, ratings and extra helmet. EV support is not publicly established. | Route/time-dependent app price; no current standard national fare. | Strongest public scale signal: company claims 5m+ daily rides across all categories and 70% bike-taxi share in April 2025. No comparable bike-only review score. [S11][S12] |
| **Ola e-Bike** | EV bike service; 2024 launch claimed three cities, 200 Bengaluru chargers and 10,000 planned vehicles. Current delivery is unverified. | Historical launch offer: ₹25/5 km, ₹50/10 km and ₹75/15 km—not a current quote. | Ola claimed 1.75m pilot rides; current active usage and bike-only reviews are not disclosed. [S17] |
| **Bharat Taxi Bike** | Driver-owned cooperative model with a Bike Didi programme. EV support is not disclosed. | Reported no in-app platform, convenience or commission fee in June 2026; current passenger fare is unknown. | Official snapshot: 990,082 registered customers, 300,000+ registered drivers and 291,665 rides across all modes. Registered does not mean active. [S18][S19] |
| **Yulu — adjacent** | Self-ride EV with keyless access, battery subscription and swapping. It is not a chauffeured bike taxi. | Self-ride/subscription price is not directly comparable with a bike-taxi fare. | Useful EV-operations benchmark; no comparable rider-service adoption or review measure. [S27] |

## Conclusion

The public evidence supports a **limited EV pilot, not a full Uber Go fleet conversion**. The decision needs matched petrol-versus-EV data for pickup time, completion rate, rider fare, driver earnings and vehicle uptime in one legal metro.
