WEEK 03 / MARKET SIZING

Three scopes. One honest estimate.

TAM, SAM, and SOM are nested boundaries. Each step asks what portion of demand your product can actually address.

1. Define demand

Choose the customer job, unit, geography, and time period.

2. Apply constraints

Limit the scope to channels, regions, and segments you can serve.

3. Test a share

Estimate adoption with evidence about alternatives, capacity, and switching.

Example: National food spending is too broad for one neighborhood restaurant. Its reachable delivery area is narrower; its plausible orders are narrower again. The Week 3 deck uses a local restaurant to teach this distinction. The numbers on a real plan need their own sources and assumptions.
