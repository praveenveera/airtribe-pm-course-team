# Product Masterclass

A static, public masterclass covering Weeks 1–7 of the course. It uses curated explanations from the fourteen structured class notes and seven session decks. The original course PDFs are intentionally excluded from the published site.

## Experience

- Seven weeks and 21 guided chapters, each with a beginner explanation, a three-step method, a continuous fictional grocery case, a common mistake, and a source trail. A week guide maps all fourteen class sessions and additional topics.
- A plain-language glossary defines recurring PM terms. External primary-source readings extend each week; they are linked and paraphrased, not reproduced.
- The selectable visual model, deck exercise, teardown, recall prompt, and applied decision follow the reading as practice.
- Extra topics show what else to study in each week.
- Week 4 includes a prioritization decision lab; Week 7 includes an interactive funnel with step and overall conversion.
- Each week has a short exercise grounded in its session deck. Week 3 adds an accessible TAM/SAM/SOM diagram.
- Each week has an applied decision case with evidence, three options, worked reasoning, a failure mode, an AI product lens, and an artifact prompt.
- A seven-part AI-first PM track complements the course with task selection, evidence and access, evaluations, human control, release gates, outcome metrics, and operation.
- A seven-stage capstone supports browser-local writing and export of a plain-text brief. Its rubric is for self-review and does not score answers.
- Keyword and context-based search across the full chapter text, week guides, glossary, deck exercises, cases, AI PM studios, and capstone stages, with direct result links. Search runs locally using weighted text matches and curated related terms; it does not call an AI service or send queries to a server.
- Keyboard-operable controls, responsive layout, and browser-local completion and capstone state.

Completion and capstone drafts stay in the browser's `localStorage`; they are not synced across devices or accounts. No sign-in, backend, AI answer generation, automated grading, or analytics collection is used. The illustrative cases are teaching scenarios, not verified product research.

## Local preview

Serve this directory with any local static server, then open its printed URL. For example:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

## GitHub Pages

The public `airtribe-pm-course-team` repository hosts the dashboard, survey, and this site. Its Pages workflow copies the curated site assets into `/masterclass/` and does not copy `learning/session-decks/`. The private course repository keeps the original PDFs. A successful deployment must be verified before treating the public URL as live.

## Content boundary

This is a learning aid. Company examples, classroom numbers, and causal explanations in the source material were not independently verified. Project research needs its own dated evidence and citations.
