# User experience — Window shopper (Kavya)

**Who:** Kavya, 26. Free evening. Not buying today. Just looking.

**Job (simple):** “Show me nice things and offers. Don’t push me to checkout.”

**Scenario:** `window-shopping` (browse home / offers; **no** add-to-bag on purpose)

**Evidence we already have:** desktop home `screenshots/web/gift-luxury-01-home.png` (same home for every logged-in visit). Assignment brief also allows “casually browsing”.

---

## What she does on this home

1. Pink **Beauty Bonanza** bar.
2. Search box: “Search on Nykaa” — useful if she later gets a goal; empty for now.
3. Categories: Makeup, Skin, Hair, **OFFERS**, Beauty Advice.
4. Big brand cards: SkinRX, Kay Beauty (celebrity), Chanel — all say **Shop Now**.
5. Luxe pouch if you spend **above ₹7000**.
6. App QR: extra 20% on first **app** order.
7. Timer: **Ends in …** (urgency).
8. Chat: “How may we help you”.

Nykaa is very good at **window dressing**. It is also very good at **nudging a buy**.

---

## How it felt

| Feeling | What happened |
|---------|----------------|
| **Smooth** | Pretty layout. Easy to tap a brand or category. Fun to browse. |
| **Confusing** | Everything is “Shop now” / sale. Hard to find a calm “just looking” mode (lookbook, Beauty Advice is there but not the hero). |
| **Slow** | Not slow to load. Mentally slow if she has to ignore pop-ups (app QR). |
| **Frustrating** | If she is logged in, home still treats her like a new hunter for deals — not “here are looks you saved”. |

---

## Buy / cart / checkout / reorder — did we test this?

| Step | Did we try? | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Buy** | **No** (on purpose). | Window shopper success = leave happy, maybe wishlist. |
| **Cart / checkout** | No. | Not her job today. |
| **Reorder** | No. | See Meera’s file. |

If Nykaa only measures “add to bag”, this persona looks like a **failure**. She might still be valuable later (brand love, next sale).

---

## What this means (PM class)

Home is built for **discovery + promo**. That helps Kavya *and* hurts Meera (replenish) and Priya (specific need).

**One number to watch:** wishlist / content clicks from home, not only conversion. Also bounce when pop-ups stack.
