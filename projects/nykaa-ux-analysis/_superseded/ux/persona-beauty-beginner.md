# User experience — Beauty beginner (Ananya)

**Who:** Ananya, 22. First real lipstick. Budget **under ₹500**.

**Job (simple):** “I want a safe everyday lipstick. I don’t want the wrong colour.”

**Scenario we ran:** `makeup-beginner`
**Platform:** Desktop site, logged in (4 Sep 2026).

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/good/makeup-shade-count-on-cards.png`

---

## What she did

1. Searched: **lipstick everyday wear**.
2. Saw **108** results.
3. Cards showed **24 or 25 shades**, **MOST REORDERED**, and huge review counts (one product had **500K+** reviews).
4. First item was an **ad** (₹439). Cheap options like MARS **₹189** sat next to it.
5. No quiz like “what is your undertone?” on this page.
6. Bag / pay not seen.

---

## How it felt

| Feeling | What happened |
|---------|----------------|
| **Smooth** | Lots of social proof. Price is clear. “24 shades” is honest. |
| **Confusing** | 24 shades is also scary. Which one is *my* everyday colour? |
| **Slow** | Search itself was fine. |
| **Frustrating** | To pick a shade she likely must open the product page. We did not complete that. |

---

## Buy / cart / checkout / reorder — did we test this?

| Step | Did we try? | What we saw |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Buy** | Tried add from list. Lipsticks often need a **shade first**. | No confirmed add. |
| **Cart / checkout** | Opened bag URL. | Blank. |
| **Reorder** | No. First purchase. | — |

**Simple takeaway:** Nykaa is good at “this is popular”. It is weaker at “this colour will suit you”. Wrong shade = **returns** later, even if today’s click looks fine.

---

## What this means (PM class)

A high add-to-bag number can still be a **bad** product if people send lipsticks back.

**One number to watch:** people who pick a shade → complete order, and return rate on lipstick.
