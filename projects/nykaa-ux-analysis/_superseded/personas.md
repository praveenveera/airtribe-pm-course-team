# Customer Personas — Nykaa Product Analysis

Use one persona per test run. Each maps to a scenario in `browser/scenarios/`.

| Persona ID | Who they are | Primary goal | Scenario | What to observe (buyer lens) |
|------------|--------------|--------------|----------|------------------------------|
| **careful-buyer** | Skincare shopper, dry/sensitive skin, budget ≤₹800 | Find safe moisturizer without wasting money | `skincare-budget` | Trust, filters, wrong-product risk |
| **gift-giver** | Buying for a friend, ₹2K–₹5K budget | Premium gift set with perceived value | `gift-luxury` | Gift look, packaging, delivery |
| **beauty-beginner** | First lipstick, unsure about shades | Everyday lipstick under ₹500 | `makeup-beginner` | Shade help, bestsellers |
| **loyal-replenisher** | Repeat Nykaa customer | Reorder known product fast | `replenishment` | Orders, reorder, speed |
| **window-shopper** | Casual browser, no buy today | Get ideas and offers, leave without pressure | `window-shopping` | Home clutter, inspiration vs hard sell |

## Persona detail cards

### Careful buyer (Priya, 28)

- **JTBD:** When my skin is dry and sensitive, I want a moisturizer I can trust — so I don't irritate my skin or waste money.
- **Anxieties:** Wrong ingredients, fake reviews, too many options
- **Success:** Confident add-to-bag within 10 minutes, under budget
- **Metrics:** Search→ATC, filter apply rate, return rate proxy

### Gift giver (Rahul, 32)

- **JTBD:** When a friend's birthday is coming, I want a gift that feels premium — so I don't look cheap or thoughtless.
- **Anxieties:** Will it arrive on time? Is it gift-worthy packaging?
- **Success:** Gift set selected with clear delivery date
- **Metrics:** Gifting category conversion, AOV, delivery SLA clarity

### Beauty beginner (Ananya, 22)

- **JTBD:** When I want to try makeup, I want a safe first purchase — so I don't buy the wrong shade.
- **Anxieties:** Shade mismatch, overwhelming choice
- **Success:** Shade selected with guidance (quiz, bestseller, reviews)
- **Metrics:** Variant selection completion, beginner funnel conversion

### Loyal replenisher (Meera, 35)

- **JTBD:** When I run out of my serum, I want to reorder in 2 taps — so I don't hunt through search again.
- **Anxieties:** Price changed, out of stock, slow checkout
- **Success:** Reorder from account/history in <3 minutes
- **Metrics:** Repeat purchase rate, time-to-reorder, cart abandonment at checkout

### Window shopper (Kavya, 26)

- **JTBD:** When I have spare time, I want to look at looks and deals — so I feel inspired, not pushed to buy.
- **Anxieties:** Too many pop-ups, FOMO timers, “Shop now” everywhere
- **Success:** Enjoy browsing; save something for later; leave without guilt
- **Metrics:** Time on home, wishlist saves, bounce without cart
