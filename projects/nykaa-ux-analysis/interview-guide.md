# Interview guide — 2 Nykaa users (10–15 min each)

**Who to pick:** anyone who is NOT in product or design and has used Nykaa (app or web) at least a few times. Flatmate, sibling, colleague from another team, friend.

**How to run it:** ask, then shut up and let them talk. Don't lead. Write their words, not your summary. Voice-note it if that's easier, then type it up.

**Ask permission:** "Mind if I take notes / record audio for a class assignment? I won't share your name if you'd rather I didn't."

---

## Questions (in order)

1. **How often do you use Nykaa, and app or website?**

2. **Think about the last time you opened it. What were you trying to do?**
   - Follow-up: did you finish it? How long did it take?

3. **What do you like about Nykaa?** (let them list; probe "anything else?")

4. **What annoys you about it?** (let them list; probe "anything else?")

5. **Has there been a time you were going to buy something and then didn't?**
   - What stopped you? (price / delivery / too many options / got distracted / found it cheaper elsewhere / just closed it)
   - Did you come back to it later?

6. **When you search for something specific, does it show you what you want quickly — or do you scroll a lot?**

7. **Anything you wish it did that it doesn't?**
