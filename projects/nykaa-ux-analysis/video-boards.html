The finding in one line

Strong at the ends.
Weak in the middle.

Nykaa is very good at getting you in and taking your money. It struggles with the part in between — turning a specific need into a confident choice.

  • DiscoverSearch, browse, badges, curation. Easy and even fun.
  • Decide696 results for a specific need. Ads 4–8× the budget on top.
  • BuyCoupons, reward points, COD, clear pricing. Clean 3 steps.
  • ReturnNo "buy again", no reorder. Logged-in home = logged-out home.

Three features, three verdicts

Where the product helps — and doesn't

  • Good

    Product cards

    Photo, price, offer price, rating, review count and a fit badge in one glance. Shortlist without opening anything.

  • Right data, too passive

    Beauty filters

    Skin type, concern, ingredient — the right model. But never pre-applied or suggested, and blocked behind a login on mobile web.

  • Bad

    Logged-in home

    Byte-for-byte the same as logged-out. No "buy again", no recently viewed, nothing personal. Nykaa knows my name and spends none of it.

What I would ship

Fix the middle first

  • P0 Guided narrowing after a concern search. One tap: skin type → concern → budget, pre-filled. Turn 696 into ~40. The filters already exist — just offer the combination.
  • P1 "Buy again" for logged-in shoppers. A reorder row on the home page and a one-tap reorder in Orders. Defends the highest-value repeat behaviour.
  • P1 Let mobile guests filter without logging in. Desktop already allows it. Ask for login at wishlist or checkout, not at the filter panel.
  • P2 Stop ranking over-budget ads above the best fit. When a search has a budget signal, keep sponsored items that are several times over budget out of the top slot.

Not now: an AI chat assistant. The filters already fit the category — the fix is to use them at the right moment, not add a new thing to learn.
