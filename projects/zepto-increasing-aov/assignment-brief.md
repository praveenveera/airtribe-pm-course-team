# Assignment Brief — Increasing AOV at Zepto

**Course:** Airtribe Product Management  
**Project:** Project 4 — Increasing Average Order Value (AOV) at Zepto  
**Scope:** Group Assignment  

---

## Assignment Structure & Required Checkpoints

### Step 1: User Research & Questionnaire (Group Activity)
**Objective:** Understand how users shop on Zepto and identify patterns influencing basket size.

**Instructions:**
Conduct either primary research (interviews, surveys, observational insights) or secondary research (industry reports, competitive benchmarking, behavioral assumptions). Then:
1. Segment users based on shopping behavior:
   - Basket size
   - Order frequency
   - Sensitivity to discounts
   - Planning vs. urgency intent
   - Category diversity
2. For each segment:
   - Describe their behavior
   - Identify how they currently impact AOV

**Required Output:**
- 3–4 user segments clearly defined
- Behavioral characteristics of each segment
- Clear indication of which segments are driving low AOV

---

### Step 2: Identify Blockers & Frame the Core Problem
**Objective:** Understand why users are not adding more items to their carts.

**Instructions:**
Answer the following questions:
- What prevents users from increasing basket size?
- At which stage (home page, search, cart, checkout) does expansion break?
- Is the issue psychological, structural, pricing-related, or experiential?
- What assumptions does the current product make about user intent?

Then, frame 1–2 clear problem statements in structured format:
> *Users currently [behavior], because [underlying reason], which results in [impact on AOV].*

**Required Output:**
- 3–5 clear blockers
- 1–2 well-structured problem statements
- Explicit articulation of root cause

---

### Step 3: Propose Solutions to Increase AOV
**Objective:** Translate insights into focused and justified product solutions.

**Instructions:**
Propose 2–3 high-impact ideas that:
- Directly target the identified blockers
- Influence basket expansion behavior
- Feel natural within Zepto's ecosystem

For each solution:
- Describe the idea clearly
- Explain which user segment(s) it targets
- Justify why it will increase AOV
- Identify the behavioral or economic rationale

**Required Output:**
For each solution include:
- Problem addressed
- Mechanism of impact on AOV
- Clear, logical justification

---

### Step 4: Prioritization Using RICE Framework (Group Decision)
**Objective:** Demonstrate product judgment.

**Instructions:**
Evaluate your proposed solutions using criteria such as:
- Expected AOV impact
- Implementation complexity
- Cost implications
- Scalability
- Risk

Identify:
- Your top solution
- Why you would prioritize it now
- What success metrics you would track

**Required Output:**
- Prioritization framework (RICE)
- Clear reasoning for final choice
- Defined KPIs (e.g., AOV uplift %, attach rate, items/order, guardrail metrics)
