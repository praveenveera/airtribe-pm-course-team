Validated High-Confidence Truths

  • Users shop quick-commerce primarily for speed and mission resolution (57.6% urgent).
  • Threshold prompts drive basket expansion, but 30.8% of users resent adding arbitrary fillers.
  • Recommendations work (35.9% attach rate) ONLY when strictly relevant to the current mission.
  • Zepto & Blinkit hold equal market share (29.3%) among urban quick-commerce users.

Remaining Evidence Gaps (To Validate in Pilot)

  • Zepto internal baseline order contribution margin per dark store.
  • Exact drop-off rate caused by handling fee additions at final payment step.
  • Attach rate differential between fresh produce vs beauty/personal care complements.
  • Long-term retention impact of free-delivery threshold changes.
Senior PM Product Strategy & Roadmap

Validated Opportunity & Solution Suite

Validated Core Opportunity Statement

Mission-Aware Basket Completion with Zero Checkout Friction

Zepto can increase profitable AOV by helping customers complete their immediate shopping mission through surfacing 2-3 relevant, available, and value-transparent complementary items right after item #1 is added—while guaranteeing a 1-tap exit for single-item urgent orders.

Product Requirements Document (PRD) Spec

PRD: Solution A — Need-Complete Add-On Shelf

User Flow & UX Trigger Rules

  • Trigger Moment: Immediately upon user adding Item #1 to cart from Search or Home.
  • UI Component: Compact bottom sheet or inline shelf showing maximum 2-3 complementary items (e.g. Milk ➔ Bread / Eggs; Tea ➔ Sugar / Biscuits).
  • One-Tap Add: 1-tap "+" button adds item with instant feedback.
  • Explicit Dismissal: "No thanks, proceed to cart" button ensures speed is never compromised.

Technical & Inventory Rules

  • Stock Real-Time Check: Only recommend items with >10 units live in the local dark store.
  • High Margin Preference: Prioritize beauty/personal care or prepared snacks if affinity score is equal.
  • Threshold Nudge: If cart is ₹30 below free delivery, recommend an item priced at ₹35 to unlock threshold seamlessly.
