What the research says now

Four derived insights from industry research, competitor mechanics, public feedback, and the product teardown.

readout · not final findings
01 · growth gate

Trust comes before basket expansion

Freshness, missing items, stock instability, and recovery friction can make users hesitate to add more—especially in perishable categories.

trust / qualitydirectional
02 · value mechanic

The basket is priced at checkout

Public same-cart snapshots show that fees and thresholds can reverse the item-price ranking. A higher AOV is not automatically a better customer or margin outcome.

price / feesdirectional
03 · product gap

Mission-aware help beats generic upsell

The strongest working hypothesis is to complete the user’s current need, then offer a few relevant additions or replenishment cues—not a broad carousel.

mission / baskethypothesis
04 · research design

Bengaluru is the learning lab

Locality changes assortment, freshness, delivery, and fees. Bengaluru has the strongest public-feedback coverage, so it is the right controlled observation location.

Research → AOV opportunity map

The product implication is shown with its commercial guardrail so we do not optimise gross AOV alone.

P0 · validate first

Need-complete additions

After the first item is understood, show 2–4 complementary items that finish the mission.

Measure: relevant add rate, removal rate, checkout completion, contribution margin.
P0 · benchmark

Value-transparent basket

Make product total, fees, threshold gap, and final payable amount easy to compare before checkout.

Measure: fee abandonment, threshold additions, switching, final basket margin.
P1 · validate later

Editable recurring list

Support planned replenishment without copying irrelevant snacks or one-off items from a previous order.

Measure: list reuse, planned-basket AOV, item relevance, repeat rate.

Competitor mechanics: what each teaches us

This is a pattern comparison, not a national quality ranking.

PlatformPublic strength / positionFriction signal to watchAOV lesson
ZeptoFast, category-led discovery with visible offers and add-to-cart cues.Fees, stock stability, freshness, recovery, and quality vary by locality.Improve guidance and trust before adding more generic merchandising.
BlinkitNeighbourhood assortment and broad quick-commerce category promise.Public feedback discusses fees, missing/damaged items, and support.Local assortment can make the next item relevant, but reliability protects expansion.
