### B. Streamlined Group Research Story
As agreed in group discussions (*"I think we can streamline it to make one, so it feels like a group project"*), the primary research ($N=102$) and secondary data are consolidated into **one unified story**:
1. **The Commercial Context:** Zepto's NRV per order is ₹394 (SEBI Filing), while competitor Instamart reaches ₹700 AOV. 85-90% of q-commerce GMV is household essentials (Bain 2026).
2. **The Shopper Reality ($N=102$):** 57.8% of orders are quick 1-item replenishment (milk, eggs) capping spend at ₹180–₹250. 70.6% notice fee thresholds.
3. **The Core Blocker:** Users treat Zepto as a fast emergency utility. Flat ₹199 free delivery thresholds create a rigid stopping point.
4. **The Product Solution Sequence:** 
   - **Phase 1:** Solution A (*Need-Complete Shelf*, RICE **864**) for 1-tap complementary items.
   - **Phase 2:** Solution D (*Progressive Reward Ladder*, RICE **493**) to replace flat finish lines.
   - **Phase 3:** Solution F (*Quick Price Comparison*, RICE **390**) for price-transparent add-ons.
