Where expansion may break

SearchAn urgent trip may begin and end with one known need.
ProductPrice or pack value may not justify an extra item.
CartThe basket feels complete at the shopper's budget limit.
CheckoutAn offer or fee can shift attention from need to threshold.
Threshold responseOf 28 Zepto respondents who noticed a threshold or fee, 16 added a useful item, 8 added mainly to unlock, 2 paid, 1 made no change and 1 removed items or left. Offers and fees may differ by city, account and date.
Root cause hypothesisUrgent trips favor speed, while discretionary additions need clear value. A generic suggestion or threshold can miss the trip's purpose. Search and checkout are proposed intervention points, not stages proven to fail by this survey; validate them with funnel data and observed sessions.

Problem statements

1 / Immediate needs

Users currently finish an urgent Zepto purchase with a short basket, because they prioritize completing the known need quickly and may miss a relevant essential, which results in lower spend per order and a possible second trip.

2 / Offers and discovery

Users currently consider an extra item and then hold back, because the final cost or value of that addition is unclear against their budget, which results in fewer useful additions to the basket.

How to read the score

RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort.

Reach
People with a matching last-trip signal in the 30-person Zepto subgroup. Groups overlap.
Impact and confidence
Planning judgments. They are not measured feature effects.
Effort
Rough person-weeks for a pilot.

Test plan and KPIs

Test the first two ideas separately, then compare their basket effect with checkout and unit economics.

08 / 08

1. Test design

Randomize eligible users. Start with the quick-checkout add-on, then test frequently bought together after an item is added.

  • Measure by trip segment
  • Show only in-stock, relevant items
  • Keep checkout easy to continue

2. Primary KPI

Merchandise AOV = net product sales after discounts ÷ completed orders.

  • Add-on take rate
  • Items and categories per order
  • Revenue per eligible visitor

3. Guardrails

A higher basket is useful only if the overall trip still works for shoppers and the business.

  • Checkout conversion and time
  • Repeat orders, refunds and cancellations
  • Contribution per order; promotion cost
