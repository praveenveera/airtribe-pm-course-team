# Five-Minute Executive Submission Script (Project 4 — Zepto AOV)

---

### 0:00–0:45 — Step 1: User Research & Segmentation ($N=92$ Empirical Dataset)

> "Hello everyone. Our project tackles how Zepto can sustainably increase Average Order Value (AOV) without compromising 10-minute delivery speed or customer trust. 
>
> We conducted primary research across $N=92$ verified quick-commerce shoppers using our Basket Stories survey app, combined with secondary industry reports from Redseer ($\$90\text{B}+$ retail) and BCG. 
>
> From the $N=92$ dataset, we identified 4 distinct user segments:
> 1. **Urgent Replenishers (57.6% of orders):** Single-item urgent buys (milk, eggs). This is our primary low-AOV driver (capped at ₹180–₹250).
> 2. **Threshold Fillers (30.8% of orders):** 70.7% notice delivery fee thresholds, actively searching for add-ons to unlock free shipping.
> 3. **Planned Household Buyers (11.6%):** Multi-category ₹600+ baskets.
> 4. **Impulsive Explorers:** High category crossover, responsive to discount bundles."

---

### 0:45–1:45 — Step 2: Blockers & Core Problem Framing

> "Why aren't users expanding their baskets? Our research revealed 3 critical expansion breaks:
>
> First, **Intent Lock on Home Page**: Users enter with surgical intent. Generic homepage carousels fail to capture mission-specific complements.
> Second, **Cart Review Break**: At cart review, recommendations lack context. 32.6% of users stop expanding due to fear of unnecessary spend.
> Third, **Threshold Friction**: 30.8% add random low-value filler items just to cross the ₹299 threshold.
>
> We framed our core problem statement:
> *'Users currently check out single-item urgent orders (57.6% of carts), because generic cross-sell recommendations fail to complement their immediate shopping mission, resulting in suppressed AOV (₹180–₹240) and high delivery cost per order.'*"

---

### 1:45–3:15 — Step 3: Proposed Solutions

> "To solve this, we engineered 3 ecosystem-aligned product solutions:
>
> 1. **Solution A: Mission-Aware Need-Complete Add-On Shelf (Primary Winner):** Triggers 2–3 exact recipe or complementary complements directly on the cart drawer (e.g., Milk $\rightarrow$ Cereal/Biscuits, Tea $\rightarrow$ Sugar/Snacks). It targets Urgent Replenishers and Threshold Fillers with zero cognitive friction.
> 2. **Solution B: Household Basket Builder:** A multi-item pantry selector offering progressive tier discounts for Planned Household Buyers.
> 3. **Solution C: Reorder-Plus Smart Cues:** Pre-populates regular replenishment items at checkout for repeat shoppers."

---

### 3:15–4:15 — Step 4: RICE Prioritization & Group Decision

> "Using the RICE framework:
> - **Solution A scored 864** ($\text{Reach}=80\%$, $\text{Impact}=2.0$, $\text{Confidence}=90\%$, $\text{Effort}=3.84$). It reaches the broadest audience and directly leverages our $35.9\%$ empirical attach rate.
> - **Solution C scored 432**, and **Solution B scored 336**.
>
> Solution A is our prioritized choice because it addresses the largest volume of low-AOV single-item carts with minimal engineering complexity."

---

### 4:15–5:00 — Execution Roadmap & Success Metrics

> "We will measure success across 3 primary metrics:
> 1. **AOV Uplift:** $+12\text{--}15\%$ on targeted single-item orders.
> 2. **Attach Rate:** $\ge 35.9\%$ conversion on the need-complete shelf.
> 3. **Items per Order:** $+1.2$ average items per cart.
>
> **Operational Guardrails:** We strictly monitor that dark-store picker time increases by less than 15 seconds per order, and checkout conversion drop stays under $0.5\%$. 
>
> Thank you!"
