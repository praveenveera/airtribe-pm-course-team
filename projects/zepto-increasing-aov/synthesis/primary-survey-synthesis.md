## 1. Executive Summary & Overview

This synthesis document pulls together the empirical findings from **92 completed participant responses** collected via the Basket Stories V2.5 survey web app. It provides a multi-dimensional analysis of quick-commerce vs. offline grocery shopping behavior, delivery threshold sensitivity, basket expansion friction, category affinity pairs, and demographic AOV patterns.

### Key Quantitative Takeaways:
* **Sample Composition ($N=92$):** $40.2\%$ Metro Cities ($N=37$) vs $59.8\%$ Non-Metro Cities ($N=55$).
* **Channel Distribution:** Quick-Commerce accounts for **$74.9\%$ of recent purchases**, led equally by **Zepto ($29.3\%$, $N=27$)** and **Blinkit ($29.3\%$, $N=27$)**, followed by Swiggy Instamart ($7.6\%$), Amazon Fresh ($5.4\%$), BigBasket ($3.3\%$), and Offline Supermarkets/Local Stores ($25.0\%$, $N=22$).
* **Shopping Mission Split:** **$57.6\%$ of orders are high-urgency or replenishment missions** (Urgent Top-Up: $39.1\%$, Ran Out of Staple: $18.5\%$). Planned Stock-Up accounts for $16.3\%$.
* **Delivery Threshold Sensitivity:** **$70.7\%$ ($65/92$) of shoppers actively notice free-delivery fee thresholds**.
  * $41.5\%$ ($27/65$) add useful items they intended to buy later.
  * **$30.8\%$ ($20/65$) add low-value filler items** solely to unlock free delivery.
  * $10.8\%$ ($7/65$) choose to pay the fee.
* **Recommendation Receptivity:** Zepto shoppers showed the **highest recommendation attach rate ($59.3\%$, $16/27$)** when recommended items feel complementary to the primary need.

## 2. Multi-Dimensional Breakdown & Cross-Tabulations

### A. Metro vs. Non-Metro City Behavior
| Metric / Behavior | Metro Cities ($N=37$) | Non-Metro Cities ($N=55$) | Strategic PM Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dominant Mission** | Planned Stock-Up ($27.0\%$) & Urgent Top-Up ($32.4\%$) | Urgent Top-Up ($43.6\%$) & Ran-Out Staple ($21.8\%$) | Non-Metro is speed/urgency driven; Metro supports multi-item pantry stock-up. |
| **High Spend (₹1000+)** | **$37.8\%$** ($14/37$) | $23.6\%$ ($13/55$) | Metro customers have higher basket spend headroom. |
| **Quick-Commerce App Share** | Zepto ($32.4\%$), Blinkit ($18.9\%$) | Blinkit ($36.4\%$), Zepto ($27.3\%$) | Blinkit leads non-metro dark store density perception; Zepto leads metro hubs. |

### B. Zepto vs. Rest (Non-Zepto Apps & Stores)
| Dimension | Zepto Cohort ($N=27$) | Rest Cohort ($N=65$) | Insight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Add-On Attach Receptivity** | **$59.3\%$** ($16/27$ added item) | $26.2\%$ ($17/65$ added item) | Zepto users are **2.2x more receptive** to complementary add-on shelves. |
| **Basket Size Distribution** | Compact (2-3 items: $44.4\%$; 4-6 items: $29.6\%$) | Larger (10+ items: $30.8\%$) | Zepto baskets are compact but open to 1-2 complementary additions. |
| **Primary Mission** | Ran-Out Staple ($37.0\%$) & Urgent ($22.2\%$) | Planned Stock-Up ($21.5\%$) & Urgent ($46.2\%$) | Zepto is used primarily as a fast replenishment channel. |

### C. Why Not Zepto & Consideration Switch Gap (Non-Zepto Buyers, $N=65$)
* **Top Drivers for Competitors/Offline:**
  1. Delivery Reliability ($27.7\%$)
  2. Local Proximity & Speed ($16.9\%$)
  3. Bulk Pack Value in Supermarkets ($13.8\%$)
  4. Catalog Availability ($13.8\%$)
* **Switching Gap ($32.3\%$, $21/65$ considered Zepto but switched):**
  * $38.1\%$ switched due to better coupon offers on Blinkit.
  * $28.6\%$ switched due to local dark store stockouts on Zepto.
  * $33.3\%$ switched due to active free-delivery passes on Blinkit Gold / Swiggy One.

### D. Online Instant Apps vs. Offline DMart / Supermarkets
| Channel Type | Dominant Basket Size | Typical Spend | How AOV is Built | Stopping Barrier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Online Instant Apps** ($N=61$) | 2 - 6 Items ($75.4\%$) | ₹200 - ₹699 ($55.7\%$) | **Delivery Threshold Nudging** (adding 1-2 items to unlock free delivery at ₹99-199) | Budget Control / Spend Cap ($38.7\%$) |
| **Offline DMart / Supermarket** ($N=22$) | 10+ Items ($63.6\%$) | ₹1000+ ($54.5\%$) | **Pack Size & Per-Unit Value** (bulk packs, volume discounts, multi-category browsing) | Need Completion ($40.0\%$) |

## 3. Category Co-Occurrence Pairs (Affinity Matrix)

Analyzing category basket combinations across all $N=92$ orders reveals the top complementary cross-sell opportunities for Zepto:

1. **`Dairy, Bread & Eggs + Fresh Produce`:** **27 basket co-occurrences** (*#1 primary basket anchor pair*).
2. **`Dairy, Bread & Eggs + Packaged Staples`:** **18 basket co-occurrences**.
3. **`Dairy, Bread & Eggs + Snacks & Beverages`:** **15 basket co-occurrences**.
4. **`Fresh Produce + Packaged Staples`:** **14 basket co-occurrences**.
5. **`Prepared Cafe Food + Beverages`:** **8 basket co-occurrences** (*#1 impulse attach pair*).

## 4. Demographic & Household Size AOV Segmentation

* **Single Living Alone ($N=23$):** Baskets concentrated in ₹200–399 ($52.2\%$). High speed sensitivity and compact 2–3 item baskets ($56.5\%$).
* **Couples / 2-Person ($N=27$):** Baskets concentrated in ₹400–699 ($33.3\%$). High threshold nudging sensitivity.
* **Family with Children (3-4 Person, $N=36$):** **$47.2\%$ spend ₹1000+** per order. High stock-up mission share ($30.6\%$) and multi-category basket breadth ($58.3\%$ are 6–10+ items).
