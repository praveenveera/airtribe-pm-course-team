## Key Empirical Findings ($N=92$ Dataset)

1. **Mission JTBD breakdown:** $57.6\%$ of orders are high-urgency staple replenishment (low-AOV drivers, ₹180–₹250).
2. **Threshold Sensitivity:** $70.7\%$ notice fee thresholds ($30.8\%$ actively add filler items to unlock free delivery).
3. **Cross-Sell Attach Receptivity:** $35.9\%$ attach rate when suggested add-ons are directly complementary to the primary item.
4. **Primary Winner:** Solution A (*Mission-Aware Need-Complete Add-On Shelf*) achieves a RICE score of **864**.
