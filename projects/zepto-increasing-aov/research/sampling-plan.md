# Project 4 — Sampling and Analysis Plan

## Team allocation

Each of the three members should recruit and conduct at least three follow-up conversations, with the survey used to widen the sample and identify participants.

Suggested recruitment mix across the group:

| Behavioural context | Target conversations | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Recent Zepto users | 4–5 | Core AOV evidence |
| Recent competitor quick-commerce users | 2–3 | Switching and alternative-app context |
| Bulk, supermarket, local-market, or subscription-led shoppers | 2–3 | Understand why quick-commerce is not the preferred basket |
| **Total** | **9–11** | Directional qualitative evidence |

These are recruitment quotas, not final segments. Final segments must emerge from observed behaviour.

## Capture for every participant

- Recent purchase method and shopping mission
- Purchase within the last 30 days
- First item or need
- Items and categories purchased
- Approximate basket range
- What was considered but rejected
- Reason for stopping
- Whether an offer, fee, or threshold was noticed in that purchase and what it changed
- Current workaround or alternative
- Exact quote or paraphrased evidence with response/participant ID
- Follow-up contact only in the restricted follow-up sheet

## Likely analysis dimensions

- Urgent top-up versus planned stock-up
- Small versus large basket
- Single-category versus multi-category basket
- Price/value-led versus convenience-led choice
- Current Zepto user versus lapsed/non-user
- Quick-commerce versus bulk/local/subscription behaviour

## Interview discipline

Ask about the most recent real purchase. Probe what happened, what the participant expected, what they considered, and why they stopped. Do not show proposed solutions before the behavioural story is complete.

## Evidence limitations to state later

- Convenience sampling may not represent all Zepto customers.
- Self-reported spend and item counts are approximate.
- Survey results cannot prove causal AOV impact.
- Non-Zepto responses explain alternatives but are not direct Zepto AOV evidence.
