# Project 4 — AOV Economics Tree

## North-star question

Can Zepto increase the value of an order while preserving conversion, margin, speed, trust, and repeat use?

```text
Order value
├── More items per order
│   ├── Relevant complements
│   ├── Threshold-driven additions
│   └── Impulse or promotion-led additions
├── Higher value per item
│   ├── Larger pack sizes
│   ├── Premium products
│   └── Better category mix
├── More categories per order
│   ├── Meal completion
│   ├── Household replenishment
│   └── Occasion or event baskets
├── More repeat orders
│   ├── Reorder support
│   ├── Subscription or routine replenishment
│   └── Membership-led frequency
└── Better economics per order
    ├── Lower discount dependency
    ├── Lower fulfilment cost per item
    └── Higher contribution margin
```

## Evidence required for each branch

| Branch | Secondary evidence currently available | Missing proof |
|---|---|---|
| More items | Offers, thresholds, recommendations, and fee mechanics are publicly documented. | Whether additions are useful, incremental, and retained. |
| Higher value per item | Competitors expose different assortment and pack-size propositions. | Whether users prefer larger packs and whether Zepto can fulfil them economically. |
| More categories | Competitors publicly promote grocery, beauty, home, electronics, and occasion categories. | Which cross-category missions are natural for Zepto users. |
| More repeat orders | Membership and replenishment mechanisms are visible in the market. | Whether reminders or memberships increase profitable frequency. |
| Better economics | Industry reports discuss speed, density, and fulfilment models. | Zepto contribution margin, discount burden, and fulfilment baseline. |

## Decision rule

Prioritise a product bet only if it can demonstrate:

1. positive basket-value movement;
2. no material conversion deterioration;
3. acceptable contribution margin;
4. no meaningful increase in complaints, refunds, or unwanted additions;
5. evidence of repeat or retained value.
