## Primary evidence needed later

### Survey evidence

The V2 survey is designed to collect directional evidence on:

- recent channel and mission;
- first need and purchased categories;
- item and spend bands;
- basket expansion action;
- considered-but-rejected items;
- stopping reason;
- observed fee/threshold effect;
- Zepto choice or alternative-method fit.

### Follow-up conversation evidence

The sampling plan targets 9–11 conversations:

- 4–5 recent Zepto users;
- 2–3 recent competitor quick-commerce users;
- 2–3 supermarket, local-market, bulk, or subscription-led shoppers.

These are recruitment targets, not current results.

### Observational evidence

When resumed, compare the same four missions across Zepto and competitors:

1. urgent top-up;
2. meal mission;
3. household replenishment;
4. weekly stock-up.

Record discovery, search, pack sizes, recommendations, fees, thresholds, availability, substitutions, and checkout friction. Do not treat a public website surface as proof of the signed-in app experience.

## Analysis rule when primary data arrives

1. Report sample size and recruitment mix.
2. Separate Zepto orders from non-Zepto alternatives.
3. Use survey data for directional frequency and interviews for explanation.
4. Preserve participant IDs and exact/paraphrased quote labels.
5. Compare findings against each secondary assumption.
6. Mark each assumption **supported, weakened, contradicted, or unresolved**.
7. Recalculate the product priority and market opportunity only after this reconciliation.
