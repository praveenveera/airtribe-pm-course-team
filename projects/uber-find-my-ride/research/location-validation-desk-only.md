Nothing here should be cited as confirmed physical fact; it's public-source context to sanity-check the desk research and survey findings against.

## Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA)

- Uber and Ola operate designated pickup zones distinct from general taxi stands — multiple public sources reference an "Ola Zone" and level-specific Uber/Ola pickup areas, but exact level numbers conflict across sources (one cites Level E car park, another Level C) [1][2][3].
- Uber's own official driver-information page for HYD is intentionally vague at the airport-specific level: it says the app "will direct drivers to designated locations" that "might be different from where you'd go if you weren't driving with Uber," but gives no exact level, zone name, or stopping-rule detail for RGIA specifically [4].
- Ola operates a staffed "Experience Zone" with personnel to help passengers locate their cab [3].
- **What this does and doesn't confirm:** it corroborates the desk-research report's existing framing (§3.3, "RGIA is a useful high-complexity case") — a controlled, zone-based pickup system exists — but does not verify current exact signage, walking distance, or whether the zone location has changed recently. Treat consistent with the report's existing hedge, not as new confirmed detail.

## Secunderabad Railway Station

- Ola, Uber, and local taxis operate around the station [5].
- South Central Railway's 2026 redevelopment introduced real operational rules: the **Platform 1 side now permits pick-up/drop only** (no idle parking), and the **Platform 10 side uses an Access Control System** with a free 15-minute pickup/drop window before overstay charges apply; basement parking is designated for longer stays [6].
- **What this adds beyond the original desk research:** this is new, specific, source-backed evidence of a time-boxed access rule (15 minutes) at a major Hyderabad transit hub — directly relevant to the project's "restrictions/rules" requirement (assignment Step 1) and to Insight 4/H3 (drivers constrained by where they can legally stop). Worth folding into the Step 1 restrictions section.
- **Gap:** no source found describing a rideshare-specific designated zone (as distinct from the general pick-up/drop rules above) or how well radio-app pin accuracy matches these two sides of the station.

## Raidurg Metro Station

- High-footfall interchange (~60,000 daily riders), located at Raheja Mindspace Junction in HITEC City, near Inorbit Mall and Mindspace IT Park — i.e., embedded in a dense mixed-use commercial cluster, not a standalone station [7][8].
- HMR (Hyderabad Metro Rail) operates a general parking facility for two- and four-wheelers near the station [8].
- **Gap:** no public source found describing a dedicated Uber/Ola pickup zone, signage, or stopping rule specific to rideshare at this station — unlike Secunderabad, nothing here beyond "parking exists nearby." This location remains the least-verified of the three, consistent with it being a comparison case rather than a priority in the original plan.

## Net effect on the project

- **Secunderabad's 15-minute access-control rule is a genuine new finding** — add it to the desk-research report's restrictions section (§3.6) with source [6].
- **RGIA and Raidurg add no material new confirmed facts** beyond what the desk research and survey already established — they mainly confirm that even public/official sources are vague at the level of detail fieldwork would have provided.
