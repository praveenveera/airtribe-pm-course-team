## Reviewed evidence notes

### YV0001 — Full arrival-to-pickup walkthrough

**Visible observations**

- `00:00–01:23`: the walkthrough begins inside the arrivals area and follows overhead exit signs.
- `02:40`: the route passes an airport-authorised transport counter visibly branded for Ola, Uber, Rapido and another cab provider.
- `02:40–03:48`: the video identifies lifts or escalators to Level 0, adding a vertical transition to the journey.
- `03:48–04:47`: the route continues through the Airport Plaza before reaching the outdoor vehicle area.
- `04:47`: the pickup area shows a vehicle queue and overhead parking-zone directions.

**Creator claim, not independently verified:** the description says the route can be confusing, especially with luggage.

**Use:** evidence that the airport journey contains multiple stages, level changes, signs, service counters, and an outdoor pickup area. Use these details to test discoverability, luggage burden, accessibility, and landmark recognition.

**Limitations:** this is a produced walkthrough, not an observed failed pickup or participant interview. It does not measure walking time, confusion, frequency, or pickup success. No transcript was available.

### YV0002 — Pickup-facility snapshot

**Visible observation:** the 12-second clip shows an organised outdoor curb area with lane separators, vehicles, and passengers waiting with luggage.

**Creator claim, not independently verified:** the description characterises the facility as efficient and easy to use.

**Use:** preserve as contradictory evidence against assuming that every airport pickup is confusing. Ask what makes the area work for some riders and fail for others.

**Limitations:** the clip does not show the route from arrivals, the Uber app, a rider-driver meeting, or a failed pickup. It cannot validate ease of use.

### YV0019 — Historical OTP-based airport pickup

**Visible observations**

- `00:30`: the video introduces OTP-based pickup for Uber Premier at Hyderabad Airport.
- `00:33`: the driver flow shows a pickup opportunity and a “Let's Go” action leading to the Uber pickup point.
- `00:37`: drivers are instructed to join a Premier queue; the video says Uber staff would assist there.
- `00:54`: the driver enters the rider's PIN when the rider is about to enter the vehicle.

**Use:** historical evidence of a queue-based, next-car pickup model that reduces dependence on finding one pre-assigned vehicle.

**Limitations:** published in 2019. Current availability, eligible categories, staffing, zone design, and Hyderabad app behavior are unverified. The channel name alone is not proof that the flow remains official or active. No transcript was available.

## Initial implications for validation and interviews

These are research probes, not interview findings:

1. Can riders discover and follow the move from arrivals to Level 0 and the Airport Plaza without external help?
2. Which part is hardest with luggage, children, age-related limitations, or reduced mobility?
3. Do riders understand the difference between the transport counter, app-cab zone, parking zones, and the actual meeting point?
4. Does the current Uber app still use a queue, PIN, assigned vehicle, or another airport-specific flow?
5. Why does one creator describe the facility as confusing while another describes it as easy?
