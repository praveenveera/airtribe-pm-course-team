# Non-API Internet Search Log

**Scope:** Hyderabad, India context, standard on-demand four-wheel passenger rides
**Search window:** 15–16 September 2026
**Outcome:** Broad public-web sweep reached the predefined saturation rule

## Method

The sweep used public pages available without login, an API key, or paid data access. Search-engine results were used to discover sources; claims were then checked against the underlying page where possible.

The unit collected was a **usable evidence record**, not a webpage count. Duplicate pages, syndicated copies, SEO summaries, and anecdotes that repeated an existing mechanism without adding a new location, segment, cause, workaround, rule, or contradiction were excluded.

## Query families covered

| Round | Query family | Representative queries | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Uber product and Hyderabad | `site:uber.com Hyderabad pickup directions airport D1`; `Uber Hyderabad airport priority access`; `Uber pickup suggested point precise location` | Current HYD airport flow, existing product tools, and feature-availability gaps |

| 3 | Railway and metro | `Secunderabad station cab pickup gate`; `Kacheguda Uber pickup`; `Raidurg metro pickup bay`; `LB Nagar metro cab traffic` | Gate/arm selection, construction, crowding, road conflict, and historical dedicated zones |

| 5 | Malls, hospitals, and gated communities | `Hyderabad mall cab pickup point`; `hospital cab pickup elderly Hyderabad`; `gated community Uber entry MyGate` | Historical mall zone, gate-verification pattern, and accessibility leads; thin current operator evidence |
| 6 | Events and busy streets | `Hyderabad event ride hailing pickup advisory`; `Parade Grounds Uber pickup`; `Hyderabad no parking cab pickup` | Temporary designated zones and road-rule constraints |
| 7 | Competitors and adjacent products | `Ola Zone Hyderabad railway`; `Grab guide to pickup`; `Lyft multi entrance pickup`; `Waymo minimize walking pickup` | Curated meeting points, pre-booking guidance, assisted handoff, and walking trade-offs |

| 11 | Public videos | YouTube site-limited searches for airport, metro, mall, and station pickup guides | No sufficiently attributable new evidence; results were sparse or duplicate |
| 12 | Saturation check | Repeated mall, hospital, campus, regulation, and video queries using alternate wording | Two consecutive rounds added no new research dimension |

## What the final rounds did not resolve

- Current mall and hospital operator pickup rules
- Current rules for specific gated communities and office campuses
- Live Hyderabad availability of Uber pickup features
- Hyderabad Uber four-wheel trip volume
- Contact, pin-change, wait, cancellation, and support-contact rates attributable to pickup coordination
- A statistically useful public-review sample

## Stopping decision

The public non-API sweep is complete for Phase 0 because more broad searching is now producing duplicates faster than new understanding. Further progress should come from live observation, approved API/export collection, venue confirmation, and interviews—not from claiming that the visible internet represents all riders.
