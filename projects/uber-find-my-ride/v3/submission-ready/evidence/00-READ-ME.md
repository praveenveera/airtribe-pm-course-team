# Uber Find My Ride — evidence pack

This package supports the final Project 3 PDF and video. It separates desk research, survey method, anonymized primary evidence, and analysis.

## Evidence boundary

- The assignment asks for at least 10 in-depth interviews.
- That requirement was not met.
- The primary research is a self-serve online survey with interview-style prompts; it must not be represented as synchronous interviews.
- The final evidence base contains 62 unique submissions after removing confirmed duplicate R032.
- The sample is directional and is not representative of Hyderabad prevalence.
- Only 4 responses came from drivers.
- The four open-text or voice prompts were optional in the live interface.
- Consent was required in the interface but was not persisted as a separate response-row field.

## Package contents

- `01-Internet-Research/` — curated competitor analysis, desk-research report, and source register.
- `02-Survey-Method/` — live URL, app details, and full-size app screenshots.
- `03-Anonymized-Responses/` — contact-safe survey workbook with 62 unique records.
- `04-Analysis/` — corrected dashboard and insight traceability.

## Privacy controls

The package excludes participant contact details, raw API payloads, mixed-schema raw workbooks, test rows, local transcription artifacts, and internal temporary files. Respondents are identified only by anonymous response IDs.

The raw internet collection workbook remains in the private project workspace but is intentionally excluded from this distributable package because public video descriptions copied into that workbook contain creator emails and phone numbers. The curated reports and source register retain the evidence links needed for review.
