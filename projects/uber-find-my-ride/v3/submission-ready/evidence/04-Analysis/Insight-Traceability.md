# Insight traceability

The final evidence workbook contains the complete editable traceability table in the `Insight Traceability` sheet.

| # | Insight | Primary evidence | Strength |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Difficulty is meaningful, not universal | 24/51 recent users; 34/62 reported no issue | Strong, directional |
| 2 | For affected respondents, the problem often recurs | 23/28 issue cases reported prior occurrence | Strong within subgroup |
| 3 | Complex environments concentrate problems | Airport 10; residential 7; mall 6; office 6 mentions | Strong pattern |
| 4 | Arrived does not mean rider and driver can meet | Wrong-pin and similar-gate reports; one detailed cross-road case | Moderate |
| 5 | Calling and messaging are the main recovery layer | Calling 17; messaging 5; calls/messages helpful in 20/28 cases | Strong |
| 6 | The rider often absorbs recovery effort | Waited 9; moved 8; driver moved helped 3 | Moderate–strong |
| 7 | Failures can create material delay | 7/10 time-tagged issue records exceeded five minutes | Moderate |
| 8 | Cancellation is multi-causal | Location overlaps with destination, fare, payment, rain, and slow approach | Moderate |
| 9 | Some respondents found no effective recovery | 6/28 selected nothing helped | Moderate |
| 10 | Finding the ride includes vehicle identification | One vehicle-number mismatch report | Weak edge case |

## Interpretation controls

- Location and action totals are multi-select mention counts.
- `23/28` means the issue happened before; it does not prove recurrence at the same venue.
- `24/51` describes recent four-wheel users in this sample; it is not Hyderabad prevalence.
- Driver-wide conclusions are not supported by four responses.
