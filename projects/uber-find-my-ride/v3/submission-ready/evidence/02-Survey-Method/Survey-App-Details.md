# Survey app details

## Live app

https://praveenveera.github.io/uber-pickup-survey/

## Purpose

The app collected directional primary research about four-wheel ride-hailing pickup coordination after booking. It is an academic research interface and is not operated by or affiliated with Uber.

## Experience

- Mobile-first single-page web app.
- English, Telugu, and Hindi interface.
- Up to 17 steps depending on rider/driver role and whether a pickup problem was reported.
- Rider and driver branches.
- Consent gate before continuing.
- Four optional open-text or voice prompts on the difficulty path.
- Structured tags for what happened, location, action, extra time, what helped, and recurrence.
- Optional follow-up contact field in the collection system; all contact details are excluded from this package.
- Timestamped submissions sent through Google Apps Script to Google Sheets.

## Evidence limitations

- The app could not ask live follow-up questions.
- Open-ended prompts were optional, so answer depth varies.
- The questionnaire schema changed during collection; earlier records contain less narrative detail.
- Consent was gated in the interface but not stored as a separate backend field; the consent screenshot is app evidence, not row-level consent proof.
- The online survey does not satisfy the assignment's 10 in-depth interview requirement.
