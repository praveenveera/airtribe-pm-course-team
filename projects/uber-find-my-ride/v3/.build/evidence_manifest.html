App details, screenshots, online survey evidence, internet research, analysis, and explicit limitations.

Method deviation: the required 10 synchronous in-depth interviews were not completed. The primary research is an online self-serve survey and is presented as directional evidence, not as interview completion.

1. Evidence base

Research method

A mobile-first online survey with rider and driver branches, a consent gate, optional qualitative prompts, and structured tags for problem type, location, action, delay, recovery, and recurrence.

Live app: https://praveenveera.github.io/uber-pickup-survey/

Controls applied

  • Confirmed duplicate R032 excluded from final analysis.
  • Contact details and raw API payload excluded.
  • Participant references reduced to anonymous response IDs.
  • Counts distinguish 51 recent four-wheel users from 62 total unique responses.
  • Multi-select counts are treated as mentions, not unique people.

Critical limitations

  • No live follow-up probing; answer depth varies.
  • Four narrative prompts were optional in the live interface.
  • Only four responses came from drivers.
  • Twenty-nine responses came from other cities, so this is not a Hyderabad-only sample.
  • Consent was required in the interface but not stored as a separate backend response field.
  • No reliable Hyderabad trip-volume or issue-rate denominator was found.
