# Uber Find My Ride Assignment

## Brief

### Problem statement

Uber is one of the most widely used mobility platforms, helping millions of users get from point A to point B quickly and reliably.

However, for many users—especially in dense urban areas—the experience after booking a ride can still feel uncertain and stressful. Once a ride is confirmed, users often struggle with questions such as:

- Where exactly should I stand?
- Will the driver find me easily?
- Should I move to a better pickup point?

In crowded locations such as malls, office complexes, airports, or busy streets, drivers may find it difficult to reach the exact pinned location. This often leads to multiple calls, cancellations, or delays.

While Uber shows a pickup point and ETA, it does not always proactively guide users toward the most optimal or realistic pickup experience in complex environments. Uber is exploring whether it can play a more active role in helping users coordinate pickups more effectively, reduce confusion, and improve the overall reliability of the pickup experience.

As a Product Manager, evaluate this opportunity and outline how you would approach understanding the problem before proposing solutions.

## Step 1 — Conduct market research and competitive analysis

- Analyze demand across user segments.
- Identify and size the target market.
- Study existing alternatives users rely on today.
- Assess restrictions, rules, and other laws.
- Assess market trends.
