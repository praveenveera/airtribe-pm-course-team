**What this is:** A fresh-eyes reframe of the requirement, research approach, and product solution, written from a senior-product-leader lens. It does not redo the underlying research — it changes what the existing evidence is asked to prove and how the three required product ideas are packaged.
**v1 is preserved, not deleted:** original files remain at [`../README.md`](../README.md), [`../scope.md`](../scope.md), [`../synthesis/02-strategy-implications.md`](../synthesis/02-strategy-implications.md), and [`../synthesis/04-product-ideas.md`](../synthesis/04-product-ideas.md).

## The one-line version

v1 answers a rider-experience question well. The bigger, better-funded question is a marketplace-economics one — and this project has almost no driver-side evidence to answer it. v2 names that gap as the headline, collapses two overlapping product ideas into one phased bet, and uses the freed-up "third idea" slot to propose closing the gap directly.
