# Submission Requirements — Uber Find My Ride

**Status:** Recorded on 17 September 2026. Treat these as non-negotiable delivery constraints.

## Required submission package

| Requirement | What the project must provide | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Written deliverable | One final **PDF only** | Not assembled |
| Video evidence | One working **Loom link or Google Drive video link** | Not recorded/uploaded |
| Presenter visibility | Presenter visible while the presentation is shown | Plan before recording |
| Duration | Strictly **under 5 minutes** | Script not drafted |
| Evidence integrity | AI may help organize and structure; insights and data must come from real, traceable research | Applied throughout the synthesis |

## What this changes

The final PDF must be built around the assignment's required structure:

1. Research Goal and Key Interview Questions
2. Top 10 Most Relevant Insights, with anonymized direct quotes and supporting visuals
3. Strategy Implications
4. Top 3 Research-Led Product Ideas
5. Evidence boundary, limitations, and source traceability

The video should explain the problem, the strongest evidence, the chosen direction, and the three ideas. It must not introduce claims, quotes, interview proof, or metrics that are absent from the PDF evidence pack.

## Final pre-submission checks

- [ ] Export and visually inspect the final PDF; submit no editable source file.
- [ ] Check every quote, count, chart, and source against its underlying evidence.
- [ ] Remove personal contact details and anonymize participant references.
- [ ] Confirm any identifiable interview image, video, or screenshot has explicit consent.
- [ ] Record a presenter-visible video under 5 minutes.
- [ ] Verify the Loom or Google Drive link opens for the evaluator without requesting access.
- [ ] Put the working video link in the PDF and the submission form, if one is provided.

## Evidence boundary

This project has survey-based, interview-style primary responses rather than ten fully probed synchronous interviews. The final PDF must state that limitation plainly and must not represent survey records as live interviews. The strongest records, direct quotes, and the contact-safe analysis are documented in [`synthesis/01-survey-insights.md`](synthesis/01-survey-insights.md) and [`data/survey/README.md`](data/survey/README.md).
