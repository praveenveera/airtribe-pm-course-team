# Phase 0 — Project Scope

**Status:** In progress  
**Last updated:** 16 September 2026

## Confirmed scope decisions

### 1. Geography

| Scope level | Decision | Why it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Broader market context | India | Provides the national mobility, market, competitor, and regulatory context |
| Primary research city | Hyderabad | Keeps interviews, pickup behavior, operating restrictions, and location examples locally relevant |

The primary research should focus on people with recent Uber pickup experiences in Hyderabad. India-wide evidence may provide context, but it must not be presented as proof of Hyderabad-specific behavior.

### Candidate Hyderabad location types to investigate

These are starting categories, not confirmed problem hotspots:

- Rajiv Gandhi International Airport
- Metro and railway station surroundings
- Shopping malls
- Office and technology campuses
- Hospitals
- Hotels and event venues
- Busy commercial streets
- Gated communities or large residential complexes

Research will determine which location types actually create the greatest pickup difficulty.

### 2. Uber ride category

| Included | Excluded |
|---|---|
| Standard on-demand four-wheel passenger rides in Hyderabad | Uber Auto and Uber Moto |

The project will study the shared four-wheel pickup journey rather than compare individual fare tiers. This keeps the research focused on locations where vehicle access, legal stopping space, gates, luggage, and rider-driver identification may overlap strongly with the assignment problem.

This fit is a **scope hypothesis**, not a research result. Interviews and market evidence must still establish whether four-wheel users experience the problem frequently and severely enough to justify intervention.

## Evidence boundary

- Hyderabad was selected by the requester as the primary research city.
- Standard on-demand four-wheel passenger rides were selected; Auto and Moto are excluded.
- No Hyderabad market research or participant interviews have been completed yet.
- The location list is exploratory and does not prove where pickup problems are most common.
- No market size, demand, competitor, legal, or product conclusion has been made.

Phase 0 is complete only when geography, ride categories, interview population, participant recency, submission format, and deadline are recorded.
