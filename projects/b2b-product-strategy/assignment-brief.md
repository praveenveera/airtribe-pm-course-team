# B2B Product Strategy Assignment

## Brief

You are the Product Manager at a B2B SaaS company building an AI-powered Customer Support Automation platform. The product competes with platforms such as:

- Intercom AI
- Zendesk AI
- Freshdesk
- Salesforce Service Cloud

The company initially targeted mid-sized businesses with 200–1,000 employees, but adoption has been slower than expected.

Most companies still rely on a combination of:

- Basic help centers
- Manual ticket assignment
- Existing CRM and ticketing systems

Leadership believes there is a significant opportunity to evolve the product from “another AI chatbot” into a comprehensive **AI-powered Customer Operations Platform**.

Your task is to define a clear product strategy for the next 2–3 years.

## Step 1: Identify the Specific Customer Segment to Target First

For example:

- Startups
- Mid-sized companies
- Enterprises

## Step 2: Define the Market Opportunity

Explain:

- How big is this opportunity?
- How frequently is the product used?
- How painful is the problem today?
- Why would customers be willing to pay for a better solution?

## Step 3: Select the Core Problem to Solve Deeply

Focus on one problem and explain why it matters most for your chosen customer segment.

## Step 4: Think About Moats and Defensibility

Explain what defensibility you can build over time. This could include:

- Data advantages
- Integrations with CRM, ERP, accounting systems, communication tools, and other business systems
- Network effects across IT teams, customer support teams, finance teams, and operations teams
- Switching costs
- Brand

Be specific about how your product becomes harder to replace as it scales.

## Step 5: Describe Your Land-and-Expand Strategy

Explain:

- How you will enter a company with a small, easy-to-adopt use case
- How you will grow your footprint across teams, departments, or use cases
- Who your initial buyer is
- How you will create internal pull within the organization
- What will trigger expansion opportunities

## Submission Guidelines

- Use real data sources for market research, such as reports, platforms, policy documents, and product pages.
- Ensure diversity in interview participants across company sizes, industries, and customer-support maturity levels.
- Do not fabricate user data or quotes.
- Keep the Loom video under 5 minutes.

## Step 6: Expected Deliverable

- Submit the assignment in **PDF format only**.
- Include a video link, using either Loom or Google Drive.

## Important

Do not jump into features immediately.

Focus on clarity of thinking:

- What problem are you solving?
- Who are you solving it for?
- Why does it matter?
- How does it scale into a durable business?
