## Recent case

### 1. Last difficult customer-support issue

A customer reported that a ticket had been closed even though the underlying issue was unresolved. The ticket status was in the helpdesk, but the relevant account and case history was stored in the CRM. The participant manually found the account, copied recent CRM notes into the ticket and contacted a second-line team through Teams.

Participant-reported workload:

- Approximately 40–60 tickets per day.
- Approximately 5–6 difficult tickets per day.
- Difficult tickets often involve repeat contact, frustration, or movement between technical and billing teams.

### 2. Systems and people involved

`Customer email → helpdesk ticket → CRM account search → manual note copy → Teams escalation to L2 → helpdesk response → Excel status tracking`

Systems: Freshdesk or Salesforce Service Cloud, Salesforce or client CRM, Outlook, Teams, Excel and an internal knowledge base.

People: Frontline associate, L2 support, technical or billing teams, manager and customer.

### 3. Where time was lost or information repeated

- Approximately 20–25 minutes per difficult case was spent collecting context from CRM, email and the ticket.
- CRM updates did not automatically update the helpdesk.
- L2 responses in Teams were not written back into the customer ticket.
- The participant normally kept four applications open.
- A prior client migration from Zendesk to Salesforce broke old ticket links, requiring searches in the previous system.

Derived estimate: 5–6 difficult cases multiplied by 20–25 minutes suggests approximately 100–150 minutes of daily context-gathering work. This is calculated from participant estimates, not independently measured.

### 4. Customer or business consequence

- Customers contacted support repeatedly because the ticket status did not represent the real outcome.
- The next agent could not see the Teams escalation and had to reconstruct the case.
- Managers required status updates every four hours from a separate Excel tracker.
- Broken historical links increased search time after tool migration.

### 5. Current workaround

- Copy recent CRM notes into the helpdesk manually.
- Escalate through Teams.
- Maintain a personal Excel escalation tracker.
- Search the previous ticketing system when historical links are broken.
