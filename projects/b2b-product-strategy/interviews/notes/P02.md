## Recent case

### 1. Last difficult customer-support issue

A customer transaction was declined because the account appeared to have an expired KYC status. The customer said documents had already been submitted, but the documents were stored in a separate document-management system. The participant could not approve the case from the email evidence and had to follow the formal risk and compliance workflow. The customer was asked to resend documents, increasing frustration.

The participant normally handles L1.5 complaint and exception cases such as KYC holds, charge disputes and blocked accounts rather than simple first-line requests.

### 2. Systems and people involved

`Customer email or call → ServiceNow case → core-banking CRM verification → document-management-system check → Pega or compliance request → secure-email response → manual attachment to ServiceNow → customer update`

Systems: ServiceNow, Pega, Salesforce or core-banking CRM, secure Outlook, compliance portal, call logger and document-management system.

People: Complaints associate, customer, risk or compliance team and other operations teams.

### 3. Where time was lost or information repeated

- Audit information had to be logged in three places, adding approximately 15 minutes per case.
- The CRM and ServiceNow were separate and not automatically linked.
- Compliance responses arrived through secure email and had to be uploaded manually into ServiceNow.
- Re-entering the customer account number across systems created repeated work.
- A repeat call could create a new IVR case ID, producing duplicate cases for the same issue.
- Formal Pega escalation could take approximately 4–6 hours.

### 4. Customer or business consequence

- Customers waited without a reliable progress update during the approval process.
- Customers could be asked to resend documents already submitted.
- Duplicate cases fragmented the customer history.
- Speed could not override audit and permission requirements.
- Manual attachment and repeated logging increased operational effort and audit risk.

### 5. Current workaround

- Open each system separately and re-enter the customer identifier.
- Raise the formal Pega or compliance workflow.
- Receive the response through secure email.
- Upload the response manually to ServiceNow for audit.
- Ask the customer to resend documents when evidence could not be verified in the approved document system.
