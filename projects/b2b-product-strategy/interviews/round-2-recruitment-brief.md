# Round 2 Recruitment Brief: Closing the Segment and Role Gaps

This is for the interviews still remaining after submission. It does not change today's submission, which is limited to the video ([PROJECT_STATUS.md](../PROJECT_STATUS.md)).

## Why this round exists

Round 1 completed 2 of the 8 minimum interviews (`interviews/README.md`), and both landed outside the target profile:

- P01 and P02 are both enterprise companies (10,000+ employees), not the 51–1,000 employee segment the strategy is testing.
- Both are frontline associates. Nobody with budget visibility (a leader or buyer) or data-access authority (IT/security) has been interviewed.
- Neither industry (outsourced BPO, retail banking) is a B2B SaaS or technology-enabled-services company.

Round 2 exists to close exactly these gaps, not to pad the interview count.

## Targeted segment

### Must-have (hard filter — do not book if these fail)

| Criterion | Requirement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Company size | 51–1,000 employees, prioritize 200–1,000 | Matches the assignment's original target; both completed interviews are 10,000+ |
| Business type | B2B SaaS or technology-enabled services | Matches the working ICP hypothesis; diversifies away from BPO/banking |
| Systems | Uses a helpdesk (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom, or similar) **and** a separate CRM or system of record | The pain being tested is cross-system fragmentation; a single all-in-one tool removes the premise |
| Recency | Handled or reviewed a difficult, cross-system case in the last 3 months | Keeps recall concrete instead of hypothetical |

### Exclude

- Companies with 10,000+ employees (already covered).
- Regulated banking/financial-services operations (already covered by P02), unless deliberately seeking a second regulated data point.
- Employees, resellers, or partners of Intercom, Zendesk, Freshworks, or Salesforce (conflict of interest).
- Anyone being pitched — this stays a research conversation, not discovery for a sale.

### Roles and quota (fills the 6 slots remaining from the original 8)

| Role | Remaining slots | What it closes |
|---|---:|---|
| Frontline support/CS agent, mid-market company | 1 | A non-enterprise data point to contrast against P01/P02 |
| Support or CS leader (Head/VP Support, Support-Ops Manager, COO) | 3 | Buyer, budget, and priority — untouched by P01/P02 |
| Support-ops/CRM admin, IT, security, or procurement | 2 | Data-access feasibility — P02 flagged this as a likely hard blocker |

### Support-maturity spread

Use the four bands from the research pack (`research/Airtribe_B2B_Product_Strategy_Assessment_Pack.pdf`, section 6). Don't let this round cluster in one band the way round 1 clustered in "integrated/agentic" territory.

| Maturity band | Signal to listen for | Target this round |
|---|---|---:|
| Reactive/manual | Shared inbox, spreadsheets, informal escalation | 1 |
| Standardized | Helpdesk, macros, static knowledge base | 2 |
| AI-assisted | Copilot/chatbot partially deployed | 2 |
| Integrated/agentic | AI wired into business systems with governance | 1 (stretch) |

### Industry spread

BPO and retail banking are already covered. Prioritize: dev-tools or martech SaaS, e-commerce SaaS, HR or fintech-for-business software, IT-managed services. Don't take two participants from the same company or the same close-knit team.

## The interview ask

Same ground rules as round 1 (`interviews/outreach-template.md`): don't share the product concept or interview questions in advance, anonymize notes, no exact quotes without consent, no leading questions.

### Direct message — frontline or support-ops, mid-market

> Hi, I'm researching how support teams at growing B2B companies handle customer issues that need information or action from more than one system — for example a helpdesk plus a separate CRM. Could I get 25–30 minutes to talk through one recent case your team handled? This is research for a product-strategy project, not a sales call, and I'll anonymize everything before writing it up.

### Direct message — support or CS leader (economic buyer)

> Hi, I'm researching how support and customer-ops leaders prioritize and budget for tools when customer context is split across a helpdesk and other systems like a CRM or billing platform. Could I get 25–30 minutes to talk through a recent case your team escalated, and how your team thinks about piloting or buying new tools? This is a research conversation, not a pitch — I won't be presenting anything for you to evaluate, and notes will be anonymized.

### Direct message — IT, security, or procurement

> Hi, I'm researching how IT, security, and procurement teams evaluate new tools that would need read or write access to systems like a helpdesk, CRM, or document store. Could I get 20–25 minutes to talk through how your team currently approves that kind of access, using a recent example if you have one? This is a research conversation, not a vendor pitch, and everything will be anonymized.

### Referral request

> Hi, I'm looking to speak with people in customer support, support operations, IT, security, or anyone who's been involved in buying or approving a support or AI tool — ideally at a company with roughly 50 to 1,000 employees. It's a 20–30 minute research conversation about one real recent case, not a sales call, and everything is anonymized. Is there someone you'd feel comfortable introducing me to?

### Screening questions (apply before booking)

1. What is your role, and does it touch customer support, support operations, IT, security, or purchasing?
2. Roughly how many employees does your company have?
3. Does your team use a helpdesk and a separate CRM or business system?
4. Have you handled, reviewed, or approved something related to a difficult support case in the last three months?
5. *(Leader/buyer contacts only)* Do you have visibility into budget or purchasing decisions for support tools?
6. *(IT/security contacts only)* Are you involved in approving data access or new vendor tools?

Do not send these questions to the participant in advance — they're for the recruiter's own screening call.
