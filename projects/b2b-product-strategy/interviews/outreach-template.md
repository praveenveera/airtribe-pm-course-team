# Interview Recruitment Messages

## Direct message

Hi, I am researching how B2B support teams handle customer issues that require information or actions across multiple systems.

Could I speak with you for 25 to 30 minutes about one recent support case? This is research for a product-strategy assignment, not a sales call. Any notes will be anonymized.

## Referral request

Hi, I am looking to speak with people who work in customer support, customer success, support operations, CRM administration or support-tool buying.

The conversation is a 25 to 30 minute research interview about one recent support workflow. I will not ask for customer-confidential information, and the notes will be anonymized. Is there one person you would feel comfortable introducing?

## Screening questions

Ask only what is needed to confirm fit:

1. What is your role in customer support or customer success?
2. Does your team use a helpdesk and a separate CRM or business system?
3. Have you handled or reviewed a difficult support case during the last three months?

Do not send the proposed product concept or interview questions in advance. That can lead the participant toward the expected answer.
