# B2B Product Strategy

**Status:** this is the active submission candidate for tonight's deadline. It supersedes the earlier draft in `../final/`, which stays on disk untouched in case you want to compare or fall back to it — nothing there was deleted or overwritten.

## What this is

A single, standalone strategy document and deck — not a comparison of drafts. It reads as one team's reasoning: diagnose why the original mid-market motion adopted slower than expected, then let that diagnosis drive segment, market, competitive position, product sequencing, moat, roadmap, and go-to-market. Every claim is evidence-labeled; every unknown is named as one.

Two prior working passes fed into this one — an evidence-report-style rewrite and a diagnosis-led reasoning pass — plus the four-vendor competitive teardown's own evidence-labeled findings (Customer/Promise/Model/Weakness per vendor, the wedge-and-vulnerability table, the crowded-capability-space finding, and the rejection-conditions/evidence-gaps table). None of that editorial history appears in the document itself; only the strategy does.
