# Airtribe B2B Product Strategy

This project develops an evidence-controlled two-to-three-year strategy for an AI customer-support company. The current direction is a hypothesis: validate one frequent cross-system support workflow before proposing a broad Customer Operations Platform.

## Evidence boundary

Public reviews, articles, product pages, pricing pages, surveys, and research papers identify patterns and hypotheses. They do not prove the exact customer segment, workflow priority, willingness to pay, or demand for a separate product.

The strategy is intentionally evidence-limited. The interviews strengthen segment fit, complex-escalation context/handoff pain and least-privilege pilot design. Do not claim interview saturation, a validated ICP, a confirmed economic buyer, willingness to pay, or pilot demand.
